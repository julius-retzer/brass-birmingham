  <behavior>
    Link Scoring (Canal + Rail):
    - Given player has a link between Birmingham and Dudley, and Birmingham has 1 flipped cotton (linkScoringIcons=1) and Dudley has 1 flipped iron (linkScoringIcons=1), link scores 2 VP
    - Given player has a link between two cities with no flipped industries, link scores 0 VP
    - Given player has a link adjacent to opponent's flipped industry (linkScoringIcons=2), that industry's icons count toward the link owner's VP
    - Given player has 3 links with varying adjacent flipped industries, total VP equals sum of all linkScoringIcons across all adjacent cities

    Industry Scoring:
    - Given player has 2 flipped industries (VP=5 and VP=9), scores 14 industry VP
    - Given player has 1 unflipped industry, it is removed during scoring (not scored)
    - Canal scoring removes ALL unflipped industries; only flipped survive

    Income-to-VP Conversion (Rail Era Final Scoring ONLY):
    - Per rules: tiebreaker is income then money, but income is NOT converted to VP
    - The `triggerRailEraEnd` must record final income and money for tiebreaking
    - Winner determined by: highest VP, then highest income, then most money

    Automatic Era Transition:
    - When nextPlayer fires and drawPile.length === 0 AND all players have empty hands, auto-transition to eraScoring
    - eraScoring entry action performs scoring (link + industry VP)
    - If era is 'canal', auto-transition to eraTransition state
    - eraTransition entry action: remove level 1 tiles, re-deal 8 cards each, reset round=1, set era='rail', reset merchants
    - If era is 'rail', auto-transition to gameOver state
    - gameOver is a final state; entry action records final scores

    First Round Rail Era:
    - After era transition sets round=1 and era='rail', the first round gives each player 1 action (not 2)
    - The existing isFirstRound guard must work for both eras (verify round===1 check applies after era transition)
